# Street festival permits
## Organizer | id=org
[start Festival planned] > [send Apply for road closure] > [send Apply for noise permit] > [send Book first aid] > [catch Closure approved | key=eventId] > [catch Noise permit issued | key=eventId] > [catch First aid confirmed | key=eventId] > [end Festival cleared]
## City Office | id=city
[start:message Closure application] > [service Check route] > [xor Route free?]
Route free? >(Yes: free = true) [send Approve closure] > [end Closure done]
Route free? >(No: default) [user Propose detour] > Approve closure
[start:message Noise application] > [service Check decibel limits] > [send Issue noise permit] > [end Noise done]
## Medical Service | id=med
[start:message First aid request] > [user Assign paramedics] > [send Confirm first aid] > [end Booked]
## Residents | blackbox
Apply for road closure ~> Closure application: Route
Apply for noise permit ~> Noise application: Stage plan
Book first aid ~> First aid request: Crowd size
Approve closure ~> Closure approved: Closure
Issue noise permit ~> Noise permit issued: Permit
Confirm first aid ~> First aid confirmed: Crew
Approve closure ~> Residents: Road notice
