# Travel booking
## Traveler Portal | id=portal
[start:message Trip requested] > [user Choose itinerary] > [send Request flight] > [catch Flight offer received | key=tripId] > [send Request hotel] > [catch Hotel offer received | key=tripId] > [send Request payment] > [catch Payment settled | key=tripId] > [end Trip booked]
Choose itinerary ..> [store Booking DB]
## Flight Desk | id=flights
[start:message Flight query] > [service Search flights] > [send Send flight offer] > [end Flights done]
Search flights ..> [store Booking DB]
## Hotel Desk | id=hotels
[start:message Hotel query] > [service Search rooms] > [xor Room free?]
Room free? >(Yes: free = true) [send Send hotel offer] > [end Hotel offered]
Room free? >(No: default) [send Send sold out] > [end Sold out]
Search rooms ..> [store Booking DB]
## Billing | id=billing
[start:message Charge requested] > [service Charge card] > [send Send receipt] > [end Billed]
Charge card ..> [store Booking DB]
## Notifications | id=notify
[start:message Booking complete] > [service Send itinerary mail] > [end Mail sent]
## Card Network | blackbox
Request flight ~> Flight query: Route
Send flight offer ~> Flight offer received: Offer
Request hotel ~> Hotel query: Dates
Send hotel offer ~> Hotel offer received: Offer
Request payment ~> Charge requested: Amount
Charge card ~> Card Network: Authorization
Card Network ~> Payment settled: Approval
Send receipt ~> Booking complete: Receipt
