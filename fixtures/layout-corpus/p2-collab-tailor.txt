# Suit tailoring
## Customer | id=customer
[start Suit wanted] > [send Send measurements] > [catch Fitting invitation | key=orderId] > [user Attend fitting] > [end Suit collected]
## Tailor | id=tailor
[start:message Measurements received] > [user Cut cloth] > [user Sew suit] > [send Invite to fitting] > [end Fitting booked]
Send measurements ~> Measurements received: Sizes
Invite to fitting ~> Fitting invitation: Date
