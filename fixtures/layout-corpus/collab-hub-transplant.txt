# Transplant coordination
## Transplant Desk | id=desk
[start:message Organ offered] > [service Screen offer] > [send Request tissue typing] > [eventgw Await typing]
Await typing > [catch:message Typing compatible | key=caseId] > [send Book surgery] > [catch:message Surgery team ready | key=caseId] > [send Dispatch courier] > [catch:message Outcome reported | key=caseId] > [end Transplant done]
Await typing > [catch:message Typing mismatch | key=caseId] > [send Decline offer] > [end Offer declined]
Await typing > [catch:timer Typing overdue | after=PT4H] > [send Escalate delay] > [end Escalated]
## Donor Registry | id=registry
[start Donor registered] > [user Verify consent] > [send Announce organ] > [send Request donor imaging] > [eventgw Await imaging]
Await imaging > [catch:message Imaging clear | key=donorId] > [send Confirm viability] > [catch:message Case closure | key=donorId] > [end Registry closed]
Await imaging > [catch:message Imaging abnormal | key=donorId] > [send Withdraw organ] > [end Organ withdrawn]
## Surgical Team | id=surgery
[start:message Surgery requested] > [user Assemble team] > [send Request blood units] > [catch:message Blood units reserved | key=caseId] > [send Report team ready] > [catch:message Organ arrived | key=caseId] > [user Perform transplant] > [send Report outcome] > [end Surgery done]
## Tissue Typing Lab | id=lab
[start:message Typing requested] > [service Run HLA match] > [xor Compatible?]
Compatible? >(Yes: score >= 80) [send Report match] > [end Match reported]
Compatible? >(No: default) [send Report mismatch] > [end Mismatch reported]
## Organ Transport | id=transport
[start:message Courier requested] > [service Plan route] > [send Notify ministry] > [user Collect organ] > [send Deliver organ] > [end Organ delivered]
## Recipient Ward | id=ward

## Blood Bank | id=blood

## Pharmacy | id=pharmacy

## Insurance Liaison | id=insurer

## Imaging Unit | id=imaging
[start:message Imaging requested] > [service Acquire scan] > [xor Scan normal?]
Scan normal? >(Yes: normal = true) [send Report clear scan] > [end Scan clear]
Scan normal? >(No: default) [send Report abnormal scan] > [end Scan abnormal]
