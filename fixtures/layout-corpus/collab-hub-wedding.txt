# Wedding planning agency
## Venue Manager | id=venue
[start:message Venue hold requested] > [service Check calendar] > [xor Date free?]
Date free? >(Yes: free = true) [send Hold venue] > [end Venue reserved]
Date free? >(No: default) [send Decline date] > [end Date declined]
## Caterer | id=caterer
[start:message Catering quote requested] > [user Plan menu] > [xor Capacity ok?]
Capacity ok? >(Yes: ok = true) [send Send catering quote] > [catch:message Catering accepted | key=weddingId] > [send Confirm menu] > [end Menu fixed]
Capacity ok? >(No: default) [send Decline catering] > [end Catering dropped]
## Florist | id=florist
[start:message Flower quote requested] > [user Design arrangements] > [send Send florals ready] > [end Florals done]
## Event Planner | id=planner
[start:message Couple briefing] > [service Draft event plan] > [send Request venue hold] > [eventgw Await venue]
Await venue > [catch:message Venue held | key=weddingId] > [send Brief vendor coordination] > [catch:message Vendors confirmed | key=weddingId] > [send Send save-the-date] > [send Request shuttle] > [end Plan locked]
Await venue > [catch:message Venue unavailable | key=weddingId] > [send Propose alternatives] > [end Alternatives proposed]
Await venue > [catch:timer Venue silent | after=P3D] > [send Chase venue] > [end Venue chased]
## Photographer | id=photographer
[start:message Photographer booking] > [service Check availability] > [send Confirm shoot] > [end Shoot booked]
## Music Band | id=band
[start:message Band inquiry] > [user Check repertoire] > [send Confirm band] > [end Band set]
## Vendor Coordination | id=vendors
[start:message Vendor briefing] > [service Compile vendor list] > [send Request catering quote] > [send Request flower quote] > [send Book photographer] > [send Book band] > [eventgw Await quotes]
Await quotes > [catch:message Catering quote | key=weddingId] > [send Accept catering] > [catch:message Florals ready | key=weddingId] > [send Confirm vendors] > [end Vendors done]
Await quotes > [catch:message Catering declined | key=weddingId] > [send Switch caterer] > [end Caterer switched]
Await quotes > [catch:timer Quotes late | after=P2D] > [send Remind vendors] > [end Vendors reminded]
## Transport Fleet | id=fleet
[start:message Shuttle requested] > [service Plan shuttle] > [send Confirm shuttle] > [end Shuttle planned]
## Guest Registry | id=guests
[start:message Guest list requested] > [user Collect RSVPs] > [send Send guest count] > [end Guests counted]
## Invitation Printer | id=printer
[start:message Invitation request] > [service Print cards] > [send Ship invitations] > [end Invitations shipped]
