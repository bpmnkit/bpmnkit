# Insurance claim handling
[annotation Claims above the review threshold need a second opinion]
## Claims Intake | id=intake
[start:message Claim reported] > [user Register claim] > [service Validate policy] > [send Request documents] > [end Intake done]
## Policy Admin | id=policy
[start:message Policy lookup requested] > [service Load policy] > [xor Policy active?]
Policy active? >(Yes: active = true) [send Return coverage] > [end Coverage returned]
Policy active? >(No: default) [send Report lapse] > [end Lapse reported]
## Damage Assessment | id=assessment
[start:message Assessment ordered] > [user Inspect damage] > [service Estimate cost] > [send Deliver estimate] > [end Assessment done]
## Fraud Check | id=fraud
[start:message Screening requested] > [service Score claim] > [xor Suspicious?]
Suspicious? >(Yes: score > 80) [user Investigate case] > [send Report finding]
Suspicious? >(No: default) Report finding
Report finding > [end Screening done]
## Claims Approval | id=approval
[start:message Approval requested] > [user Review claim] > [xor Approved?]
Approved? >(Yes: approved = true) [send Authorize payout] > [end Claim approved]
Approved? >(No: default) [send Notify rejection] > [end Claim rejected]
## Payment Service | id=payment
[start:message Payout authorized] > [service Prepare transfer] > [send Request bank transfer] > [catch Transfer confirmed | key=claimId] > [send Confirm payment] > [end Payment done]
## Legal Review | id=legal
[start:message Legal input requested] > [user Assess liability] > [service Draft opinion] > [send Send opinion] > [end Legal done]
## Customer Service | id=service
[start:message Customer inquiry] > [user Answer inquiry] > [send Send status update] > [end Inquiry closed]
## Archive | id=archive
[start:message Archive request] > [service Store documents] > [service Index record] > [end Archived]
