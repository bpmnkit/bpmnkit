# University admissions
## Admissions Office | id=admissions
[start:message Application submitted] > [service Check completeness] > [send Request transcript check] > [send Request language score] > [eventgw Await verification]
Await verification > [catch:message Transcripts verified | key=applicantId] > [send Forward to faculty] > [catch:message Faculty decision | key=applicantId] > [send Issue offer] > [end Offer issued]
Await verification > [catch:message Transcripts invalid | key=applicantId] > [send Reject application] > [end Application rejected]
Await verification > [catch:timer Verification stalled | after=P10D] > [send Chase verification] > [end Chased]
## Applicant Portal | id=portal
[start Applicant registers] > [user Submit application] > [send Submit documents] > [eventgw Await response]
Await response > [catch:message Offer letter | key=applicantId] > [user Review offer] > [send Accept offer] > [end Offer taken]
Await response > [catch:message Rejection letter | key=applicantId] > [end Rejection noted]
## Faculty Review Board | id=faculty
[start:message Faculty review requested] > [user Review application] > [send Report faculty decision] > [end Review done]
## Scholarship Committee | id=scholarship
[start:message Scholarship screening] > [user Rank candidates] > [send Award scholarship] > [end Scholarship awarded]
## Housing Office | id=housing
[start:message Housing requested] > [service Allocate room] > [xor Room free?]
Room free? >(Yes: free = true) [send Confirm room] > [end Room assigned]
Room free? >(No: default) [send Deny housing] > [end No room]
## Transcript Verification | id=transcripts
[start:message Transcript check requested] > [service Verify records] > [xor Records valid?]
Records valid? >(Yes: valid = true) [send Confirm transcripts] > [end Records fine]
Records valid? >(No: default) [send Flag invalid transcripts] > [end Records flagged]
## Visa Support | id=visa
[start:message Visa support requested] > [user Prepare visa letter] > [send Send visa letter] > [end Visa done]
## Student Finance | id=finance
[start:message Tuition plan requested] > [service Compute tuition] > [send Report tuition plan] > [end Finance done]
## Orientation Team | id=orientation
[start:message Orientation slot requested] > [user Plan session] > [send Confirm orientation] > [end Orientation planned]
## Enrollment Services | id=enrollment
[start:message Offer accepted] > [service Open student record] > [send Request tuition plan] > [send Request housing] > [eventgw Await readiness]
Await readiness > [catch:message Tuition plan ready | key=studentId] > [send Request orientation slot] > [catch:message Orientation booked | key=studentId] > [send Confirm enrollment] > [end Enrolled]
Await readiness > [catch:message Housing denied | key=studentId] > [send Offer off-campus list] > [end Off-campus advised]
Await readiness > [catch:timer Readiness overdue | after=P14D] > [send Send reminder] > [end Reminder sent]
