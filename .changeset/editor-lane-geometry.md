Divide into lanes splits an unlaned own pool into equal bands, as bpmn-js does, when each band keeps 60px: the pool keeps its size, nothing moves and every node joins the lane its centre lies in; a smaller pool still grows by one band per new lane. The `addLane` op takes an optional `count` for this. A lane edge drawn through node centres can be dragged off them in the direction that keeps them in their lane. A node crossing the own pool's top or left edge (or lying over its name strips) grows the pool and its lanes up or left; growth onto another pool there refuses the drop. Widening the own pool, by growth or by a resize drag, pushes the pools beside it right, and those beyond them, with their content, instead of stopping at them; a black box still stops at the pool beside it. A vertical own pool grows only right and down. Shrinking a pool or lane from its bottom pulls what is below up by as much, so grow then shrink returns the drawing to where it was; it stops where a shape above would be hit. Lane bands drawn without a pool hold and grow around their nodes as a pool does: a node crossing their edge grows them, one whose centre leaves them is refused. Data objects and nodes in no lane, drawn below such bands, move freely outside them, and the bands do not grow onto a pool above or left of them.
