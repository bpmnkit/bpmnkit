# Getting started — Open Optimize

From Camunda Hub, you can open Optimize in any [environment](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/concepts/environments) you have access to:

1. Log in to Camunda Hub.
1. In the left navigation, click **Environments**, and then select an environment. Each environment has its own instance of Optimize.
1. Under **Applications**, on the **Optimize** card, click **Open**. This opens Optimize for the environment in a new tab.

You can also expand an environment in the left navigation, and select Optimize from its applications.

**Tip**
If the environment is paused, you must [resume it](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/hub/organization/manage-environments/index#resume-a-paused-environment) to access its applications.
