# Manage your connectors

In Camunda Hub, you can monitor and manage the connectors running on your cluster on the Connector Management page.

Monitor and manage connectors running on your cluster.


## About connector management

With cluster connector management, you can monitor and manage running connectors on your cluster. This includes inbound connectors, such as [webhooks, message queue subscriptions, and polling subscriptions](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/reference/glossary#inbound-connector), and outbound connectors.

Use this feature to review connector status and troubleshoot issues. For example, you can inspect connector details and use the [activity log](#activity-log) to investigate failures.
