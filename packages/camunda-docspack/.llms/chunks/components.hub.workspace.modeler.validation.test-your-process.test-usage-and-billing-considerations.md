# Test your process — Test usage and billing considerations

The use of Test mode may result in additional charges depending on your organization's [plan](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/saas/organization/manage-plan/available-plans) and the type of cluster you are using. To avoid extra costs, follow these guidelines based on your plan:

- **Enterprise plan:** Use a [Basic cluster](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/saas/clusters#cluster-type) for non-production testing to avoid costs. For further assistance, [contact Camunda support](https://camunda.com/services/support/).
- **Free trial plan:** You can use any cluster. See [Free Trial clusters](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/saas/clusters#free-trial-clusters).
