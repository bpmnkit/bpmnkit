# Integrate IDP into your processes

Integrate intelligent document processing (IDP) into your end-to-end processes in Camunda Hub.

Integrate your published document extraction and classification templates into your end-to-end processes in Camunda Hub.

**Tip**
New to IDP integration? See the [example IDP integration](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/hub/workspace/modeler/idp/idp-example) for a worked example of a simple IDP process.


## About IDP integration

When you publish a [document extraction](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/hub/workspace/modeler/idp/idp-document-extraction) or [document classification](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/hub/workspace/modeler/idp/idp-document-classification) template, IDP generates a connector template that you can apply to tasks in your BPMN diagrams. Each published template appears as a selectable element template in Camunda Hub.
