# Organization in SaaS — Activity and usage

See what happens in your organization and how much of your plan you use.

- [View organization activity](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/saas/organization/view-organization-activity): See user and cluster activity.
- [View usage history](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/saas/organization/usage-history): Monitor the usage of your organization over time.
- [View usage alerts](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/saas/organization/usage-alerts): Get notified when usage reaches a threshold.


## Plan and billing

Choose and manage your plan.

- [Create an account](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/saas/organization/manage-plan/create-account): Set up your Camunda 8 account.
- [Available plans](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/saas/organization/manage-plan/available-plans): Compare the plans and learn how to upgrade.
- [Update billing reservations](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/saas/organization/manage-plan/update-billing-reservations): Manage your hardware packages and reservations.
