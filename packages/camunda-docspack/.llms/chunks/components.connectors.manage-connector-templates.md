# Manage connector templates

Manage your connector templates in Camunda Hub.

Connector templates are a specific type of [element template](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/concepts/element-templates). To manage them, see the [manage element templates](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/hub/workspace/modeler/element-templates/manage-element-templates) documentation.
