# Camunda app integrations — Get started — self-managed

**Info**
The Self-Managed setup first requires installation and configuration by your organization's administrator, using the CLI. See [Install](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/camunda-integrations/app-integrations/installation) for more details.

Once your administrator has deployed the app, it appears in your workspace's app directory. Add it from there, or from a direct link your administrator shares with you.
