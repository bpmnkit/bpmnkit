# Technical concepts

Learn more about Zeebe's underlying technical concepts, including the architecture, clustering, partitions, internal processing, and more.

This section gives an overview of Zeebe's underlying technical concepts.

- [Architecture](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/zeebe/technical-concepts/architecture) - Introduces you to the internal pillars of Zeebe, as well as interfaces for external systems to interact with Zeebe.
- [Batch operations](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/zeebe/technical-concepts/batch-operations) - How distributed batch operations work in the Zeebe engine.
- [Clustering](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/zeebe/technical-concepts/clustering) - Discusses the internal structure and properties of a Zeebe cluster.
- [Partitions](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/zeebe/technical-concepts/partitions) - Sheds light on how Zeebe achieves horizontal scalability.
- [Internal processing](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/zeebe/technical-concepts/internal-processing) - Explains the basics of Zeebe's event processing.
- [Process lifecycles](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/zeebe/technical-concepts/process-lifecycles) - Expands on the event processing concept and goes into more detail regarding the lifecycles of selected process elements.
- [Protocols](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/zeebe/technical-concepts/protocols) - Explains how external clients communicate with Zeebe.

In addition to these sections, you may also be interested in our [Best Practices](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/best-practices/best-practices-overview).
