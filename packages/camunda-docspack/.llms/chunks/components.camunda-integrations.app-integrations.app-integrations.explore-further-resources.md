# Camunda app integrations — Explore further resources

**Tip**
To send messages to Microsoft Teams or Slack from a process, use the [App Integrations connector](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/connectors/out-of-the-box-connectors/app-integrations). The app and the connector complement each other: the app lets users act on Camunda tasks from inside the chat tool they use, while the connector lets a process post into it.
