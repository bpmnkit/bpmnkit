# Audit operations

Learn how to audit operations.

Audit [operations](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/audit-log/overview) in Camunda 8 Admin.


## Prerequisites

To follow the steps in this guide, you must be [authorized to view operations in the audit log](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/audit-log/overview/access-control).


## Audit operations

In Admin, you can audit all [`ADMIN` operations](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/audit-log/overview/recorded-operations#admin-operations):

1. In the top navigation, click **Operations log**.
2. To sort the log, click a column header.
