# Glossary — Audit log

The [audit log](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/audit-log/overview) is a record of operations, including who performed them, when, and on which entities. Use the audit log to prove compliance, meet governance and regulatory requirements, maintain operational integrity and transparency, and troubleshoot issues.
