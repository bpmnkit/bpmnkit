# Audit operations

Learn how to audit operations.

Audit [operations](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/audit-log/overview) in Camunda 8 Operate.


## Prerequisites

To follow the steps in this guide, you must be [authorized to view operations in the audit log](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/audit-log/overview/access-control).


## Audit all operations

In Operate, you can audit all [`DEPLOYED_RESOURCES` and `USER_TASKS` operations](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/audit-log/overview/recorded-operations) in the general operations log:

1. In the top navigation, click **Operations > Operations log**.
2. To sort the log, click a column header.
3. To see the details of a particular operation, click the info icon at the end of the row.
4. To filter the audit log, expand the **Filter** panel on the left side of the page. Audit log filters are under the **Operation** header.
