# Troubleshoot app integrations — Tasks not displayed

- Check you are connected to the correct Camunda cluster.
- If notifications are not shown, ensure notifications are enabled for your platform and verify that the relevant [notification rules](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/camunda-integrations/app-integrations/notification-rules) are configured for the channel, personal chat, or direct message.
- This could be due to an expired Camunda session or missing permissions. Sign out and sign in again.
