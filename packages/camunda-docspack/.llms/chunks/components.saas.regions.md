# Regions

After creating a cluster, specify a region for that cluster. Read on for details of Google Cloud Platform regions currently supported in Camunda 8 SaaS.

When you [create a cluster](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/saas/clusters/create-cluster) in Camunda 8 SaaS, you must specify a region for that cluster.

The following regions are available for customers on Trial and Enterprise plans. Enterprise customers can also [reach out to Camunda](https://camunda.com/contact-us/) to discuss custom regions.

**Info**
See [Data locations](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/saas/data-locations) for more information about where your Camunda 8 SaaS data is located and how data is handled.
