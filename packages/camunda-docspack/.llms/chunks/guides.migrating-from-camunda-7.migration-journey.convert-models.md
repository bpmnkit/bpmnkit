# Migration journey — Convert models

Your BPMN and DMN models need to be adjusted.

The [Migration Analyzer](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/guides/migrating-from-camunda-7/migration-tooling/index#migration-analyzer) can do most changes for you. Depending on how you refactor your code and what elements of Camunda 7 you have used, you can extend or customize the diagram convertion to suit your needs.

You can dive into the [technical details of model differences](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/guides/migrating-from-camunda-7/migration-tooling/index#extending-the-conversion-logic) if you are interested in more detail.
