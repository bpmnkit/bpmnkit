# Property reference — Configuration of the `restapi` component — Environments and Physical Tenants

Camunda Hub creates environments from your clusters and their Physical Tenants. To declare Physical Tenants, override their components, and see how Camunda Hub determines the status of an environment, see [environments and Physical Tenants](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/self-managed/components/hub/configuration/environments).
