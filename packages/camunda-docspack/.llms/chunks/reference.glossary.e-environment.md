# Glossary — E — Environment

An environment is a named deployment target where a team runs [processes](#process) in [Camunda Hub](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/hub/index). It's the operational unit for deployment, while the [cluster](#cluster) is the administrative unit. An environment is backed by a [Physical Tenant](#physical-tenant) on Self-Managed 8.10 and later, or by the whole cluster on SaaS and on earlier versions. Organization admins assign environments to workspaces, and every project in a workspace can deploy to the environments assigned to it.
