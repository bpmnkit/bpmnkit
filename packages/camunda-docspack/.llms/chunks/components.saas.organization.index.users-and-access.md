# Organization in SaaS — Users and access

Decide who can sign in to your organization and what they can do.

- [Organization ownership](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/saas/organization/organization-ownership): Learn how ownership works and how to transfer it to another user.
- [Create and manage users](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/saas/organization/create-manage-users): Invite users to your organization and manage them.
- [Manage user groups](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/saas/organization/manage-user-groups): Organize users in groups.
- [Manage resource-based authorizations](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/saas/organization/resource-based-auth): Control access to individual resources. This is a legacy feature.
- [Connect to an identity provider](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/saas/organization/external-sso): Let users sign in with your own identity provider.
