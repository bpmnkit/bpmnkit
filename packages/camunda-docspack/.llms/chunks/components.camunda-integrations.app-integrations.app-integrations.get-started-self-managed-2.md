# Camunda app integrations — Get started — self-managed

**Info**
The Self-Managed setup first requires installation and configuration by your organization's administrator. See [Install](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/camunda-integrations/app-integrations/installation) for more details.

1. Open **Microsoft Teams**.
2. Open **Apps** and search for **Camunda** in the **Built for your org** section.

**Note**
Your administrator may have renamed the app. Contact them if you cannot find it.

3. Click **Add** to install the app.
