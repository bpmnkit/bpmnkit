# Workspaces

A workspace is a collaboration space in Camunda Hub where a team works on projects and deploys them to the environments assigned to it.

A workspace is a collaboration space in Camunda Hub. It represents a team or business domain, and it brings together the people, the [projects](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/concepts/projects), and the [environments](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/concepts/environments) that the team works with.


## Example

A payments team might have a `Payments` workspace:

- **Members**: The developers, analysts, and reviewers of the payments team, each with a workspace role.
- **Projects**: A `consumer-loan-approval` project and a `refund-handling` project.
- **Environments**: A `payments-dev` environment for testing, and a `payments-prod` environment that an organization admin assigned to the team.
