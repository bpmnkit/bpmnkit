# Troubleshoot app integrations — self-managed

- The app is provisioned during [installation](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/camunda-integrations/app-integrations/installation) and appears in the **Built for your org** section of Microsoft Teams Apps.
- If the app is not visible, check with your IT administrator.
