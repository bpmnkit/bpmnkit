# Integrate a built-in connector — Observe your running process

After the [user task](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/guides/getting-started-orchestrate-human-tasks) **Upload receipt** is completed in [Tasklist](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/tasklist/introduction-to-tasklist), an email is automatically sent to the address you specified in the SendGrid connector's properties panel.

In [Operate](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/operate/userguide/basic-operate-navigation#open-operate), you will now see the process move forward to **Review receipt**.
