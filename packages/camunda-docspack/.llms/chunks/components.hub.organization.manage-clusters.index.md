# Manage clusters

View the clusters that host your deployment environments, and learn how clusters are created and managed in SaaS and Self-Managed.

A cluster is the infrastructure that runs Camunda 8 and hosts your [environments](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/concepts/environments). Learn how clusters relate to environments in [clusters](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/concepts/clusters). How you work with clusters in Camunda Hub depends on your deployment.


## Permissions

Only organization owners, admins, and DevOps users see the **Clusters** page. Other users work with the environments assigned to their [workspace](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/concepts/workspaces), and they don't see clusters.
