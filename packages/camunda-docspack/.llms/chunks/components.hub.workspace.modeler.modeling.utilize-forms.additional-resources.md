## Next steps

When building a form for a process, you can also use the [Filepicker form component](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/modeler/forms/form-element-library/forms-element-library-filepicker) to allow users to upload files. Learn more in [building a form for document upload](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/document-handling/upload-document-to-bpmn-process#build-a-form-for-document-upload).

You can also use the [document preview component](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/modeler/forms/form-element-library/forms-element-library-document-preview) to display and allow document download with your form. Learn more in [building a form for document preview and download](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/document-handling/display-and-download-document#build-a-form-for-document-preview-and-downloading).

Additionally, review the Camunda Academy course on [using the AI-assisted form builder](https://academy.camunda.com/c8-h2-ai-form-builder).
